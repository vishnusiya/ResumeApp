AI Resume Studio 
1. Product Overview

AI Resume Studio is an AI-powered resume creation and optimization platform that helps users create, merge, tailor, edit, analyze, and export professional resumes from resumes and Job Descriptions.

2. Key Capabilities
Capability	What It Does
AI Resume Analysis	Analyzes resume content, skills, experience, and gaps
AI Resume Improvement	Improves sections, achievements, action verbs, and impact
Multi-Resume Merge	Combines multiple resumes into one optimized resume
JD Generation	Creates professional JDs from a role/title or input
JD Matching	Aligns resume content with target JD requirements
ATS Optimization	Provides ATS compatibility score and identifies missing keywords
Live Resume Editor	Real-time editing with live A4/Letter preview
PDF & DOCX Export	Generates professionally formatted resumes
